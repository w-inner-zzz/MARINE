MARINE GNSS Guard

Can the ship trust its satellite position? Every second, ten checks decide, and when the answer is no the ship keeps navigating on dead reckoning, radar, depth soundings and other aids. Set in the Gulf of Finland, Baltic Sea.

  • Bridge view

    For navigators and pitches: ship status, instructions, the ten checks and a live map. Pick a scenario (jamming, spoofing, receiver failure) and a correction method, then press Play.

  • Teaching website

    How it works: signal inspector, check cards, playbooks with sources, maps, a briefing and the derivations behind every number.

How a problem is diagnosed

Demonstration only, not for navigation. Every voyage, signal and attack here is simulated and pre-computed; the depth chart is synthetic unless stated otherwise. Nothing is sent from your browser.
